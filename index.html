Data Science Research Program — Colab Notebook

Data science becomes much more meaningful when students use it to answer questions they genuinely care about. As Director of the National High School Data Science Research Program, I designed interactive Python notebooks that take students from their first lines of code to independent research projects — combining instruction, guided practice, and open-ended exploration. Taught to 100+ high schoolers, many with no prior programming background, across a 5-week program.
